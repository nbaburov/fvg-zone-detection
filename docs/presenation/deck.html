04
The data · and why it's hard

9 years of hourly SPY. Two real traps.

Trap 1: accuracy lies

1.8% bullish gap 1.3% bearish gap 97% no gap

The trap: always say "no gap" and you're 97% accurate and completely useless. So I score the rare class, not accuracy.

Trap 2: no peeking at the future

Leak caught

Found a popular open-source library quietly peeking one bar ahead. Results looked great and meant nothing. Catching that mattered more than any single model.

Only ~117 truly independent examples on hourly bars (60-bar windows overlap heavily). Small-data regime.

Honest: XGBoost's hourly 0.738 is still the single best score overall. The gap narrowed, it didn't vanish. Part of XGBoost's 5-min drop is that its hand-built features were tuned for hourly bars. Compare within a timeframe, not across.

09
Honest about limits

Good, bad, and ugly.

Good

Detects the pattern reliably. The science is solid: rigorous split, real leak caught, reproducible across 5 seeds and 5 models.

Strong detector. Real results.

Bad

As a money-making signal the edge is thin. With realistic trading costs, almost everything lost money.

Detection is not profit.

Ugly

Only one setup came out positive: 15-minute SPY, fixed 2R exit. Doesn't generalise to other stocks or timeframes.

One setup. Not a strategy.

A strong detector and an honest study: not a get-rich strategy, and I won't pretend it is.
